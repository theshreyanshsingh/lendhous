# Florida Deadlines Should Set LendHous's Next Builds

LendHous does not need to copy the national lenders. It needs to finish the Florida work none of them do, then close a short list of draw, capital and broker gaps. Competing lenders and private-lending software now match LendHous on quoting, portals and broker white-labelling. They lead on loan structures (ARMs, interest-only, coverage below 1.0, credit lines), photo-only draws, AI document intake, escrow, servicing plumbing and investor records. But two hard Florida dates come first. **NFIP authority runs out on December 11, 2026, and Citizens requires flood cover on all its multiperil home policies with wind cover from January 1, 2027** (condo-unit policies aside), while LendHous asks for flood only in zones A and V. **Florida doc stamps and intangible tax add about 0.55% to every loan**, and LendHous's quote hides them inside a flat 2% closing-cost line. After those, the best gaps are a fast photo-only draw path guarded by reused-photo checks, an investor-type and accreditation record that keeps loan sales on the right side of Chapter 494 and securities law, and a downloadable loan tape, because two of the largest buyers of these loans changed or are changing owners in 2026. **Thirty of the thirty-nine ranked gaps need no vendor and no outside decision to build.** The rest wait on a vendor, GT's pricing, GT's decision on sending data to Anthropic, or the attorney. The research also corrects earlier facts: Figure now owns Kiavi, Pretium owns Anchor, NYMT owns Constructive, Vaster already lends on non-warrantable condos in Miami, and Altaloans' own site says nothing about remote closings.

## How to read the marks

The research ran on 2026-11-09 across six note sets: national lenders, private-lending software, capital and brokers, draws and risk data, Florida law and market, and AI. **C** marks a company's own page or filing, or a statute. **R** marks a third party. **[snippet]** means the fact came from a search summary, not the page itself. **[1 source]** marks a third-party claim with no second source. Unless two sources are cited, every competitor fact rests on one page; a company's own page shows what it sells, not how well it works. **Effort** is relative: **S** extends an existing record, screen or nightly job; **M** adds a new record type or workflow; **L** adds money flows, servicing changes or a new product. **Waits on** names what must happen first: nothing, a vendor (or the bank), GT's pricing or another GT decision, "GT's AI decision" (sending data to Anthropic), the attorney, or the CPA. Facts about LendHous's own code come from a check of the code on 2026-11-09 and override the notes where they differ.

## Thirty of the top thirty-nine gaps can be built today

The list ranks value to a Florida private lender against effort. Ranks 1 to 30 need no vendor and no outside decision to build. Ranks 31 to 39 are the best of the gated work. Florida deadlines come first because they are cheap and dated. Draw speed and photo fraud come next: investors on forums say they would pay a higher rate for a better draw process ([BiggerPockets](https://www.biggerpockets.com/posts/7274079), R [snippet]), and LendHous already collects photos line by line. Capital-side records follow, because loan sales and partner money carry legal exposure. Broker visibility, construction controls and smaller Florida rules fill the rest.

### Build now, ranks 1 to 12

**1. Florida insurance deadlines: an NFIP lapse watch and Citizens flood rules** (built 2026-11-09: `lib/florida-insurance.ts`)
- **What:** A nightly check of NFIP authorization. In a lapse, it shows a banner and holds flood-zone closings that rely on a new or renewing NFIP policy, unless a private flood policy is in place. In the insurance file: a Citizens multiperil personal policy needs flood in every zone (dwellings of $400K or more now, all others from January 1, 2027; HO-6 and wind-excluded policies are exempt). The DSCR is stressed for a Citizens non-primary renewal at up to +50%. Citizens master policies on condo associations and 5+ unit buildings get a flag for a likely move to surplus lines.
- **Who has it:** No lender or vendor in the notes. The law drives it. NFIP is authorized only through December 11, 2026, and FEMA stops selling and renewing policies in a lapse ([FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/flood-insurance/rules-legislation/congressional-reauthorization), C). The flood phase-in is in [627.351(6)(aa)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.351) (C). Non-primary Citizens policies may rise up to 50% a year, against a 15% cap for primary homes ([Citizens 2026 rate bulletin](https://www.citizensfla.com/-/20251106-2026-rate-change), C). Condo associations and apartments lose new Citizens eligibility when a surplus-lines insurer offers comparable cover at no more than 15% above Citizens' total cost, through a clearinghouse due by January 1, 2027 ([ch. 2026-150](https://laws.flrules.org/2026/150), C). Private flood is allowed ([627.715](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.715), C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** Both dates fall within eight weeks. LendHous's rules won't ask a Citizens-insured zone X rental for flood, yet Citizens will require it. Citizens still held **254,836 policies** on September 30, 2026 ([Citizens](https://www.citizensfla.com/-/20260930-policies-in-force), C). Storms have been quiet since 2024's $124.1B of U.S. damage, with $1.1B so far in 2026 ([NHC 2024](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/index.php?season=2024&basin=atl), C; [NHC 2026](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/index.php?season=2026&basin=atl), C). These calendar events are the nearer risk.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. The insurance file records carrier and wind cover and requires flood only in zones A and V. Storm mode watches the hurricane center, not the NFIP.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing.

**2. Florida closing-cost math in place of the flat 2%** (built 2026-11-09: `lib/florida-taxes.ts`; the estimate's total stays the pricing's percentage, with the taxes split out of it; the title premium is still to come)
- **What:** Doc stamps on the note and mortgage ($0.35 per $100, rounded up; no cap on mortgages), intangible tax (0.2%, on the Florida-secured share) and deed stamps on purchases ($0.70 per $100; in Miami-Dade, $0.60 plus a $0.45 surtax unless the deed transfers only a single-family home). Show them on the quote, term sheet and closing statement. Flag a modification that adds principal for tax on the increase, once someone reads 201.09(1). Add the title premium later.
- **Who has it:** None of the Florida lenders checked (Altaloans, LendSimpli, Vaster, Capital Funding). Rates: [Florida DOR](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/doc_stamp.aspx), [201.08](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/201.08), [199.133](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/199.133), [199.135](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/199.135) (all C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** It is **about 0.55% of every loan**: $1,050 of stamps and $600 of intangible tax on a $300,000 loan. All parties to the document are liable whoever agreed to pay, and the late penalty reaches 50% (DOR, C). Title premiums are set by rule, so they can be exact too ([627.782](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/627.782), C).
- **LendHous today:** None. Closing costs are a flat percentage of the price (`pricing.assumptions.buyClosingCostPct` in `lib/quote.ts` `cashToCloseParts`), 2% by default.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing. The title premium waits on the rate schedule in rule 69O-186.003, which the research couldn't read.

**3. Reused-photo detection across draws, loans and properties** (built 2026-11-09 for draw and photo-link photos: `lib/photo-checks.ts`; property photos and camera-only links are still to come)
- **What:** Match photos by exact hash, then by perceptual hash, across every draw, loan and property photo ("same photo as draw 2 on another loan"). Flag photos with missing or mismatched capture data. Make the contractor and photo links camera-only, like the draw panel.
- **Who has it:** Truepic flags reused or manipulated photos automatically ([Truepic blog](https://www.truepic.com/blog/truepic-for-private-and-construction-lending), C) and checks that "each photo is unique" ([Truepic](https://www.truepic.com/vision/construction-lending), C [snippet]). Its customers include Kiavi, Anchor and Boomerang ([Truepic](https://www.truepic.com/draw-inspection-software), C). A national lender's servicing VP says borrower photos from another project were "flagged immediately" (same blog, the vendor's own story [1 source]).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** It guards the photo-only path in rank 4 and every storm-mode photo request.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. Photos pass location and time checks (200 m, 48 hours). Property photos store a SHA-256 hash, but nothing compares them. The photo guide still allows gallery uploads.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing.

**4. A photo-only desk-review draw path with its own fee and clock** (built 2026-11-09: `lib/draw-review.ts`; it uses the same 24-hour decision clock as other draws)
- **What:** An eligibility rule (single unit, the line's size, the borrower's history, no flags), a "desk review" inspection result (accept, adjust the percent, or require a site visit), a fee per path in Settings, and the borrower's choice per draw. Chapter 713 holds still apply.
- **Who has it:** CIVIC's Snap Draws cost $300 and pay in about one business day; an inspector draw costs $270 plus a $30 wire fee and takes three business days at best. So CIVIC sells speed, not a saving ([CIVIC](https://civicfs.com/draws/), C). Kiavi says "most draws are released without in-person inspections" ([Kiavi llms.txt](https://gist.githubusercontent.com/kiavimarketing/53134cb6f0e1b842fed5fcdedc3b9e07/raw/78b8cd3d5a5fafbb14b6b0460c2d14a8840fd7cf/gistfile1.txt), C). LendingOne triages draws for virtual inspection ([LendingOne](https://lendingone.com/core-resource/how-to-complete-a-virtual-draw-inspection/), C). Sitewire's contractors review Longhorn's borrower photos "within hours" ([Longhorn](https://www.longhorninvestments.com/longhorn-investments-sitewire-partnership), C), and Built sells remote Inspector Review ([Built](https://getbuilt.com/inspection-services/consumer-inspections), C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** On-site inspections take about two business days, so LendHous's 24-hour draw target fits only the photo tier. A desk review needs no site visit, so LendHous can price it below an inspection, which CIVIC doesn't do.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. A draw needs a recorded inspection, and staff can record observed percentages from the borrower's photos. There is no distinct path, fee or eligibility rule.
- **Effort:** S–M. **Waits on:** nothing (an owner sets the fees in Settings).

**5. An investor-type and accreditation record that gates access**
- **What:** For each partner and buyer: entity type, basis (accredited investor, licensed lender, REIT and the rest of the 494.001 list), self-certified or verified, the date and the expiry. Teasers, bids, loan sales and share moves stay blocked unless the status is current.
- **Who has it:** SyndicationPro hides e-sign and wire instructions until accreditation is verified ([SponsorCloud](https://help.sponsorcloud.io/how-to-verify-an-investors-accreditation-in-syndicationpro), C). InvestNext issues letters "within 12 hours" ([InvestNext](https://www.investnext.com/?p=3191), C). Lendr tracks accreditation with expiry ([Lendr](https://joinlendr.com/features), C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** Selling a mortgage loan to a noninstitutional investor is licensed activity, and an accredited investor counts as institutional ([494.001](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/494.001), C). Courts have held participations sold to institutions with resale limits not to be securities, though the test turns on the facts ([Loeb & Loeb](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/publications/2024/03/syndicated-loans-are-still-not-securities), R). LendHous's partners and buyers can be individuals.
- **LendHous today:** None. No record of investor type or accreditation exists.
- **Effort:** S–M. **Waits on:** nothing to build. The attorney sets which statuses gate what. Verified letters need a vendor (Parallel Markets or VerifyInvestor).

**6. A downloadable loan tape, blind and full**
- **What:** CSV or XLSX plus JSON, with a fixed, documented column set. The full version opens only after the NDA. Add as-is value, price, budget, holdback funded and left, LTC, LTARV, LTV, DSCR, credit band, completed projects, guarantor, lien position, dates, extensions and modifications, a 12-month payment string, flood zone and insurance expiry.
- **Who has it:** Lendr exports loan tapes ([Lendr](https://joinlendr.com/features), C). KBRA calls full loan-level diligence critical ([NPLA](https://nplaonline.com/blog/inside-kbras-view-how-rating-agencies-are-evaluating-dscr-and-rtl-securitizations), R [1 source]). Setpoint takes in lenders' data tapes ([ABF Journal](https://www.abfjournal.com/setpoint-launches-industry-standard-to-stop-double-pledging/), R [1 source]). Figure's "Adaptor" AI will standardize originators' data, starting with Kiavi's loans ([Kiavi press](https://www.kiavi.com/press/figure-acquires-kiavi), C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** Kiavi's loans and Toorak's Tampa platform both changed or are changing hands in 2026. A Florida originator that sells loans needs more than one buyer and a tape that travels.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. The sale tape (`tapeOf` in `lib/loan-sale.ts`) shows on screen only. Nothing downloads.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing.

**7. Property-tax status on every funded loan**
- **What:** For each funded property: parcel ID, the year's bill, paid or unpaid, and the date checked. A nightly reminder to check unpaid parcels in delinquency season, and a work-queue item.
- **Who has it:** The Mortgage Office with Cotality Tax Tracking watches non-escrowed loans across 22,000+ tax authorities ([TMO](https://www.themortgageoffice.com/cotality/), C). AFR monitors delinquent taxes and can pay them for the lender ([AFR](https://afrservices.com/services/loan-servicing/real-estate-tax-service), C [snippet]).

Florida's tax calendar wasn't sourced; check it before building.
- **LendHous today:** None. Taxes appear only as a collections advance.
- **Effort:** S–M. **Waits on:** nothing (a data feed can come later).

**8. Vacant-land and absentee-seller fraud controls**
- **What:** Flag vacant-land collateral and sellers who live elsewhere or own the lot free and clear. Add a title-checklist item that the closer verified the seller's identity by an independent method. Give processors a red-flag list: a seller-chosen or mail-away notary, no video or phone contact, a price well below market, proceeds to an account not in the seller's name. Optionally, send a certified letter to the owner's tax-roll address.
- **Who has it:** Title firms, not lenders. Capital Title now verifies ID on every vacant-land sale through Closinglock ([Closinglock](https://www.closinglock.com/how-capital-title-insurance-agency-caught-7-vacant-land-seller-fraud-attempts-with-closinglock), C [snippet]).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** In ALTA's 2026 survey, **59% of title firms saw a seller-impersonation attempt, up from 28% in 2024, and 82% named vacant land as the main target** ([Florida Realtors](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/09/seller-impersonation-fraud-attempts-rise), R; [Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/seller-impersonation-fraud-attempts-have-more-than-doubled-since-2024/), R). LendHous makes ground-up loans, and a ground-up purchase starts with a lot.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. The price-jump check, title checklist and wire call-backs exist.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing. The "well below market" flag needs an AVM (rank 38).

**9. A title date-down before each draw**
- **What:** Through the title company's existing link, ask for a date-down or a search since recording before each draw (or each draw over a threshold). Hold the draw until it comes back clean.
- **Who has it:** Built lets lenders "request title updates instantly" ([Built](https://getbuilt.com/features/risk-mitigation/), C [snippet]). Land Gorilla updates title before each draw (C in the November 7 scan; landgorilla.com returned 403 this round).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** It catches liens recorded after closing before more money goes out. Built's own service exhibit says county lien data is "not warranted to be complete or error-free" ([Built exhibit](https://getbuilt.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/B4L-Service-Features-Exhibit-v.4.25.pdf), C [snippet]).
- **LendHous today:** Partly. The title link and checklist exist; draws don't ask for a date-down.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing (the title company's fee per update is unknown).

**10. PDF tamper signals, with no AI**
- **What:** Read each uploaded PDF's metadata: creating and producing software, creation against modification dates, incremental saves. Flag editor fingerprints and dates after the statement date. Show them beside the duplicate and price-jump flags.
- **Who has it:** Inscribe checks "formatting, structure, metadata, and contextual signals" ([Inscribe](https://www.inscribe.ai/ai-risk-agents), R [snippet]).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** Inscribe reports 1 in 16 documents fraudulent and a fivefold rise in AI-made forgeries from April to December 2025 ([AWS blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-inscribe-uses-amazon-bedrock-to-stop-document-fraud-in-seconds), R [1 source, snippet]). LendHous's funds-to-close condition is a bank-statement upload. No data leaves the server.
- **LendHous today:** None. It is planned inside the fraud checks; the balance arithmetic waits for the AI reader.
- **Effort:** S. **Waits on:** nothing.

**11. SB 264 screening on borrowers, guarantors, partners and buyers**
- **What:** Ask domicile and nationality for each borrower, guarantor and 25%+ owner, against the list in 692.201(3): China, Russia, Iran, North Korea, Cuba, the Maduro regime of Venezuela and Syria. A China domicile without citizenship or permanent residency is a hard stop, unless it fits the one-home exception (2 acres or less, more than 5 miles from a military base, bought by a holder of a non-tourist visa or asylum papers). For other country-of-concern principals, check for property within 10 miles of a military base or critical infrastructure. Collect the buyer's affidavit as a title condition. Run the same test on partners and buyers who could take title at foreclosure.
- **Who has it:** None of the Florida lenders checked. The law: [692.204](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/692.204), [692.203](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/692.203), [ch. 2026-66](https://laws.flrules.org/2026/66) (all C). The Eleventh Circuit let it stand on November 4, 2025 ([Legal 500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-states/real-estate-and-construction/eleventh-circuit-permits-florida-restrictions-on-property-ownership-by-certain-foreign-nationals-to-go-forward), R [1 source]; the opinion wasn't read).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** South Florida lenders court foreign nationals: Altaloans, Capital Funding and Vaster all lend to them ([Capital Funding](https://capitalfunding.com/), C). Forfeited title vests in the state "subject to bona fide lienholders", so the lender's risk is mainly credit and title. But a barred person who takes property through foreclosure must sell within 3 years, and that rule reaches any partner or buyer who is barred and takes title at REO.
- **LendHous today:** Partly. A citizenship flag (U.S., resident, foreign) and foreign-national documents exist. No domicile, country list, proximity check, affidavit or REO test.
- **Effort:** M. **Waits on:** nothing, except the proximity check, which needs a map source the notes didn't find.

**12. A condo building check**
- **What:** For condo collateral: habitable stories; the certificate-of-occupancy year and the computed milestone due date (30 years, or 25 where the local agency requires it, such as near salt water; then every 10); milestone and SIRS uploads; paused reserves; open special assessments; association loans or credit lines; the master policy's carrier (Citizens or surplus lines); the website posting for 25+ unit associations; HOA dues and assessments in the DSCR. The result is a building status: current, repairs pending or overdue.
- **Who has it:** Vaster lists milestone inspections, SIRS reserve studies, HOA finances, insurance and dues among its condo underwriting factors ([Vaster](https://blog.vaster.com/condo-loan), C). Altaloans asks for a condo questionnaire ([Altaloans](https://altaloans.com/), C).
- **Why it matters in Florida:** Miami-Dade condo sales fell about 20–25% in 2025, with about 14 months of supply ([Vaster, citing Miami Realtors](https://blog.vaster.com/condo-loan), R [1 source]). The milestone rule ([553.899](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/553.899), C) and HB 913's SIRS, reserve-pause and association-loan rules ([ch. 2025-175](https://laws.flrules.org/2025/175), C) decide a building's cash needs. Any non-warrantable condo program needs this first.
- **LendHous today:** None. Condo is only a property type, though HOA dues already feed the DSCR.
- **Effort:** M. **Waits on:** nothing (no milestone-registry data source was found).

## Conclusion

The research changes the frame for LendHous. If the notes are right that new owners bring cheaper money, rivals will press harder on rate. LendHous's better ground is certainty a Florida borrower, broker or partner can see: closing taxes computed to the dollar instead of hidden in a flat 2%, insurance that stays compliant through Florida's 2027 changes, and a draw paid on photos within a day. Ranks 1, 2 and 4 deliver that certainty, rank 3 guards the photo draws, ranks 5 to 12 protect the book and the capital behind it, and none of the first twelve needs a vendor. The calendar sets the first moves: December 11 for the NFIP and January 1 for Citizens flood. February 1, 2027, when listings start showing taxes on the asking price, is the deadline for rank 22.

Three decisions unlock most of the rest. If GT splits the AI decision by sensitivity, approving entity papers, binders, bids, appraisals and draw photos before bank statements, the document reader, the draw pre-check and an MCP connector can start without waiting for the hardest case. One property-data contract (RentCast is the cheapest fit) turns on the below-market seller flag, REO-debt cross-checks and value-drift checks at once. And one attorney session on Chapter 494's reach, YSP disclosure and business-purpose escrow would release YSP, deferred points and escrow, and tell GT whether selling loans to individuals needs a license. The investor-type record in rank 5 is the cheapest guard on the capital side. It costs little now and protects every future loan sale, whatever the attorney decides.
