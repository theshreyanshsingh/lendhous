# Phone and Design Review, November 21, 2026

LendHous already works on a phone. A check of 78 pages, signed in as every demo seat at 390px (an iPhone 14) and at 768px (an iPad), found **no page that scrolls sideways** and no form field small enough to make an iPhone zoom in. The project's own phone check found two things sticking out at 320px (the smallest iPhone SE width), both now fixed. Staff, borrowers, partners, buyers and brokers each get a bottom tab bar on a phone, and tables fold into labelled cards. The problems were in what each screen puts first, how small some targets are, and wording slips. This pass fixed eighteen of them. The rest are ranked below.

## How it was checked

A script signed in as each demo seat and opened its pages at 390×844 and 768×1024. On each page it measured sideways overflow, tables wider than the screen, tap targets under 24px (the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum), fields under 16px and text under 12px. It also scanned the text for raw dates (2026-11-12), wrong plurals ("1 points") and broken values (NaN, undefined). Full-page screenshots of the main screens were then reviewed by eye. `npm run phone:check` covers overflow at 320, 375, 430 and 768px.

## Fixed in this pass

1. **A loan's open tab was off screen on a phone.** A link from the work queue (Closing, Servicing) opened the right tab, but the tab row stayed scrolled to Numbers & Ratios. The row now scrolls the open tab into view and fades the edge that has more tabs.
2. **Back links were 17px tall.** "← Pipeline", "← My loans" and the others are now 44px targets on a phone, with an arrow icon.
3. **The header picture squeezed the loan's name.** On a phone it took 96px of a 358px card, pushing the address onto three lines and cutting off the team pickers. It is now a 64px thumbnail there; desks are unchanged.
4. **"$0k".** Amounts under $1,000 in the short format now read "$0" or "$450".
5. **"1 points"** in a broker's fee list now reads "1 point".
6. **Raw dates.** Payment rows ("Late from 2026-11-12"), extensions, a loan's next payment, a sold loan's maturity, a buyer's purchase date and the term sheet's deadline now read "Nov 12, 2026".
7. **Too many black buttons on the borrower's documents.** Each needed paper had a full-width primary button, so a loan needing six showed six. The one "upload them all" button stays primary; each paper's own button is an outline, except one the team sent back.
8. **"Withdraw this application" sat under the loan's headline numbers.** It is now at the foot of the page, in its own dashed box.
9. **A broker's deals were last on "My deals".** They followed nine panels of tools, about 5,000px down on a phone. The deals now come straight after the totals, and the tools follow under "Tools to bring in more deals".
10. **Loan servicing opened with the maturity desk.** Its headline numbers (loans, balance, due each month, past due) now come first.
11. **A borrower's home put offers before loans.** The referral offer and the phone-notifications prompt now come after the loans.
12. **Calculator dollar fields showed 250000.** The home page's quick check and the calculators page now show 250,000.
13. **The audit log was 30,000px tall on a phone.** It showed 200 events at once. It now shows 50 a page (about 8,100px on a phone), with Older and Newest links that keep the filter.
14. **Small text buttons were hard to hit.** On a touch screen, buttons such as "Edit", "Add to calendar" and "Download (CSV)" get a hit area at least 24px tall without moving anything, and every disclosure's summary line is at least 24px.
15. **Chart month labels were cut off ("Ja…").** January now shows its year on a second line.
16. **A loan's header took a full phone screen before its tabs.** On a phone it now shows what identifies the loan (number, address, borrower, target closing, badges, next step); the team pickers, broker, repeat record and showcase open under "Loan team and details". Desks are unchanged.
17. **Long button labels pushed past a 320px screen** (the title company's "Confirm the seller's identity was checked"). Below 640px a button's label now wraps inside it; a button that fits is unchanged.
18. **The loan tape's pickers were wider than a 320px screen** on Loan sales. Each now shrinks to the row.

## Room for improvement, ranked

Ranks weigh how often a screen is used, on a phone, against the effort. **S** is a change to one screen; **M** touches a shared component or several screens; **L** is a new pattern.

| # | What | Where | Why it matters | Effort |
|---|------|-------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | Split the pipeline on a phone | Staff Pipeline | "Your queue" (10) and "For the team" (23) come before the stage tiles and the loan list. A three-way switch (My queue · Team · Loans) would show one at a time. | M |
| 2 | One money field everywhere | Staff forms | Some fields show commas (a loan's numbers), others don't (Record a payment shows 3895.83). The new site field (`app/(site)/amount-input.tsx`) could become the shared one, with cents. | M |
| 3 | An index on long pages | Reports (10,600px on a phone), Pricing (7,600px), Settings (3,800px) | A row of jump links or tabs under the title, the way a loan page has tabs. | S each |
| 4 | Sections on the borrower's loan page | Borrower loan page (4,100–7,600px) | Overview, Documents, Money and Messages as tabs on a phone; the bottom tab bar's Upload and Messages already jump there. | M |
| 5 | Destructive actions that look destructive | Declare default, Decline, Release past holds | They are styled like any outline button. A red outline and a confirm step would set them apart. | S |
| 6 | Say why a button is disabled | Send, Save, Record inspection, Approve | A grey button with no reason leaves people guessing. A line under it ("Enter a reason to decline") fixes it. | S each |
| 7 | Ask for notifications in context | Borrower home, broker home | The prompt now comes after the loans; better still, offer it right after a first upload or a first message. | S |
| 8 | Smaller teaser drawings on a phone | Partner deal feed, buyer list | The drawing takes about 40% of each card, so a phone shows one deal per screen. | S |
| 9 | Make the instant quote the hero's first button | Home page | On a phone, "See your terms" sits below "Discuss your project" and the leverage figures. It is the lower-effort step. | S |
| 10 | Raise 11px helper text to 12px on phones | Many panels | Legible, but small for older readers outdoors. | S |
| 11 | Check dark mode contrast | Tinted badges (warn on warn/12) | Not measured in this pass. | S |
| 12 | Run an accessibility audit | Every page | Contrast, labels, focus order in sheets, reduced motion. axe-core in the phone check would catch most of it. | M |
| 13 | One heading style for cards | Borrower loan cards, broker deal cards, partner and buyer teasers | Their headings are bold sans; page titles and a loan's own header are the serif. | S |
| 14 | Matching disclosure markers | Home page questions | Default triangles, where the portal uses + and −. | S |

Found in passing: a link to a loan tab that the loan's stage doesn't offer (for example `/admin/deals/d1?tab=Closing` on a loan still in underwriting) shows that tab's panel with no tab selected. It should fall back to the first tab. No link in the app was seen doing this.

## What not to change

- **Native date and select fields.** They show the phone's own picker, which beats any custom one.
- **The tab bars.** Four or five tabs with counts, plus Menu, is the right shape for each role.
- **Tables that fold into cards.** The `stack-table` pattern already labels every value on a phone.
